# Research-integrity v0 batch

This batch implements deterministic incubation utilities for the eight research-integrity projects in the BU1LD 64 queue:

- FTR-040 — Claim-Evidence Neural Auditor
- FTR-580 — Reproducibility Drift Monitor
- FTR-593 — Paper Result Consistency Engine
- FTR-603 — Autonomous Figure Auditor
- FTR-601 — Novelty Claim Stress Tester
- FTR-569 — Evidence Leakage Detector
- FTR-584 — Public Claim Boundary Checker
- FTR-622 — Counterexample Provenance Graph

These are small deterministic baselines only. They do not make research-validity, novelty, or publication-readiness decisions on their own. Their purpose is to create testable interfaces that can later be benchmarked against human-reviewed cases.
