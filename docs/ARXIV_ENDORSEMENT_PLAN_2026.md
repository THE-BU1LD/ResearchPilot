# arXiv Endorsement Plan — 2026

## Current policy
As of January 21, 2026, an institutional email address alone is no longer enough for a new submitter to qualify automatically. A new submitter generally has two paths:

1. **Existing arXiv authorship path** — use an institutional/research email and claim ownership of an existing arXiv paper in the relevant endorsement domain.
2. **Personal endorsement path** — obtain endorsement from an established arXiv author with endorsement privileges in the relevant endorsement domain.

arXiv staff do not waive the requirement or personally endorse authors.

## Best strategy for this portfolio
Do **not** request endorsement before one manuscript is clean enough to show an endorser. The fastest credible path is:

1. choose the first genuinely submission-ready paper;
2. select the primary arXiv category based on the manuscript's real contribution;
3. begin a new arXiv submission and let the system determine whether endorsement is required;
4. if required, save the endorsement-request email/link/code generated by arXiv;
5. prepare a concise endorser packet;
6. identify a small set of legitimate eligible endorsers through relevant recent papers;
7. contact a few at a time, personalized to their work;
8. stop outreach immediately after endorsement is obtained.

## Endorser packet
Prepare these before contacting anyone:

### 1. Manuscript
- clean PDF;
- no placeholder metadata;
- title/abstract consistent with actual evidence;
- limitations visible;
- negative or mixed findings stated plainly;
- citations checked.

### 2. Reproducibility page
One page maximum:
- repository URL;
- frozen commit/tag;
- exact reproduction command;
- dataset identity;
- seed/protocol summary;
- headline result table;
- known limitations;
- what is **not** being claimed.

### 3. Research identity
- ORCID if available;
- institutional/school affiliation stated truthfully;
- public GitHub/research profile;
- concise description of prior relevant research work.

### 4. arXiv request
Use the exact endorsement request generated after starting the submission. Do not invent a code in advance.

## How to find eligible endorsers
arXiv's own recommended workflow is:

1. find recent arXiv papers closely related to the manuscript, preferably papers already cited by it;
2. open the abstract page;
3. use **“Which authors of this paper are endorsers?”**;
4. identify authors who are genuinely close to the subject;
5. use the contact information made available through arXiv/author pages;
6. prefer people you already know or who have a real research connection to the paper.

### Target pool size
Build a ranked list of about **8–12 possible endorsers**, but contact only a small batch at a time.

Rank by:
1. already knows you / has reviewed your work;
2. cited directly and methodologically close;
3. adjacent researcher with clear subject fit;
4. broad field match only.

Do not mass-email the whole list. arXiv explicitly warns against emailing large numbers of endorsers at once or repeatedly emailing the same person.

## Outreach sequence

### Wave 0 — warm path
Ask first:
- existing research mentors;
- professors who have already reviewed or discussed the work;
- collaborators/coauthors who are eligible;
- external validators who genuinely inspect the manuscript and are eligible.

### Wave 1 — 2 to 3 highly relevant authors
Send a short, individualized request. Include:
- why their work is directly relevant;
- 1–2 sentence description of the paper;
- link to manuscript;
- link to reproducibility packet;
- the arXiv-generated endorsement request/link;
- an explicit statement that endorsement is only if they are comfortable with the paper's subject fit.

### Wave 2 — next 2 to 3
Only if Wave 1 does not produce endorsement. Do not resend repeatedly to silent recipients.

### Stop condition
At least one positive endorsement is required for the relevant endorsement category/domain. Once the requirement is satisfied, stop endorsement outreach.

I am preparing a preprint on [one-sentence topic]. Your work on [specific paper/topic] is directly relevant to the methodology/context, which is why I am reaching out specifically to you.

The paper studies [2 concise sentences]. The current result is [positive/negative/mixed] and I have kept the claims bounded to the evidence. Here are the manuscript and reproducibility materials:

- Manuscript: [link]
- Code/reproduction packet: [link]
- ORCID/research profile: [link]

arXiv has asked me to obtain endorsement for [category]. If you are eligible and comfortable that the work belongs in this subject area, the arXiv-generated endorsement request is here: [official request link/code]. I completely understand if you prefer not to endorse.

[Name]
[truthful affiliation]

## What not to do
- no purchased endorsements;
- no public bounty for endorsement;
- no fake affiliation or institutional email;
- no mass mail merge;
- no repeated pressure on the same researcher;
- no asking someone to endorse without letting them see enough of the work to judge subject fit;
- no hiding negative results to make the request look stronger;
- no category shopping solely to find an easier endorsement path.

## Pre-endorsement readiness gate
Do not contact endorsers until all boxes are true:

- [ ] final title and abstract;
- [ ] author list/order approved;
- [ ] category chosen from the manuscript's actual contribution;
- [ ] PDF compiles cleanly;
- [ ] no placeholders;
- [ ] canonical results frozen;
- [ ] claim-to-evidence audit passes;
- [ ] repository/reproduction link works from a clean environment;
- [ ] limitations section is explicit;
- [ ] ORCID/profile links prepared;
- [ ] arXiv submission started and official endorsement request generated.

## LAM-JEPA endorsement sprint

### Gate A — paper readiness
- obtain at least one external review/reproduction report;
- finish author/affiliation metadata;
- finish release/license/citation metadata;
- compile the arXiv source cleanly;
- freeze a submission commit/tag;
- generate final PDF + checksum.

### Gate B — category decision
Default candidate: `cs.LG`, unless the final paper's dominant contribution is clearly another category.

### Gate C — start submission
Create the arXiv submission, select the category, and determine whether the account is already endorsed for it. If not, use the official endorsement request produced by arXiv.

### Gate D — warm endorsers first
Check every external validator/reviewer and existing research contact for eligibility. A person who has actually inspected the work is a much stronger endorsement target than a cold name.

### Gate E — citation-neighbor endorsers
From the 10–20 closest cited arXiv papers, identify eligible authors using arXiv's endorser lookup. Rank by scientific closeness and contact only a few at a time.

## Success criteria
The goal is not merely to obtain a code. The goal is to have a paper that an active researcher in the field can look at and comfortably say: **this is a legitimate research contribution appropriate for this arXiv subject area**.
